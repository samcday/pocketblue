sdm670-google-sargo first-pass boot contract

This archive intentionally contains no fastboot flash script. A loader such as
U-Boot or PocketBoot is expected to consume the normal PocketBlue layout:

  images/fedora_esp.raw     EFI System Partition filesystem image
  images/fedora_boot.raw    XBOOTLDR filesystem image containing BLS artifacts
  images/fedora_rootfs.raw  root filesystem partition image

The three images may be written to separate LVs. The initramfs contains
dracut's lvm module, but deployment-specific activation and root arguments are
loader policy and are not hard-coded into the distro image.

For a root image written to an LV, preserve the root=UUID= option emitted in
the BLS entry and add the activation request at the last mile:

  rd.lvm.lv=<volume-group>/<root-logical-volume>

Dracut enables rd.lvm by default when rd.lvm.lv is present. Naming the exact
LV avoids activating every LV in the volume group.
